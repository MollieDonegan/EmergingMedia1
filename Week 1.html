In Defense of the Poor Image (by Hito Steyerl, 2011)

I found Hito Steyerl's Poor Images (2011) piece really revealing in understanding the complexity around 'the image," in the context of the world today. With rapid advances in technology and society evolving constantly, this essay provides a critical perspective on how images function in our digital age.

Within the essay, she does not present 'the poor image," as a perfect alternative to commercial media or capitalism. She highlights the contradiction that the poor image has the ability to resist commercial exploitation while simultaneously being entangled within capitalist systems. This nuance is important for understanding the role of accessible media.

Reading her essay made me think of a work I saw this year in Venice by artist Yuyan Wang called Boring Billion. The single channel video was composed purely from found footage online. What was once considered disposable or low-quality content becomes the material for new artistic expression, challenging our assumptions about image quality and value.
